`main.py` writes one short poem about agency — the power to stop and to stay — to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic, no network.

> The car reads the crossing,
> slows,
> and waits.
>
> The old woman crosses
> one slow step at a time.
>
> The light turns green.
> The car stays.
